1. **Legal Instrument Selection**: Choose the formal legal structure (LLC, C-Corp, Delaware Statutory Trust) and applicable securities exemptions (Regulation D 506(c), Rule 144A, Regulation S, or Reg A+ Tier 2).
2. **Audited Standards & Components**: Replace demonstration skeletons with certified, audited ERC-3643 / ERC-1400 standards.
3. **Regulated Identity / Compliance Integration**: Integrate an accredited OnchainID / identity registry with verified KYC/AML and sanction screening.
4. **Transfer Restrictions**: Program lockup schedules, jurisdiction whitelists, accredited investor caps, and freeze capabilities.
5. **Issuer & Transfer Agent Authority**: Formalize dual control between the Issuer (Mauro Lomibao Dar III Treasury) and an SEC-registered Transfer Agent (DTCC FAST #9450).
6. **Independent Security Audit**: Commission a comprehensive external audit from top-tier audit firms (e.g. OpenZeppelin, CertiK, Trail of Bits) with clean sign-off.
7. **Emergency Controls**: Thoroughly test pause, freeze, recovery of lost keys, key rotation, and corporate actions execution.
8. **Chain Finality & Reorganization Policy**: Formalize finality depth requirements (e.g., 64 blocks on Ethereum L1, 256 on L2) to eliminate reorg risk.
9. **Custody & Settlement**: Establish qualified custody with an SEC/FINRA-compliant custodian and DTCC FAST electronic link.
10. **Statutory Registrations**: Complete Form BD with FINRA and Form ATS / Form ATS-N with the SEC. An on-chain contract address is NEVER a security registration, exchange approval, or license.
